Parts tested on the bench

VCO tested

Crystek CVCO55BE-0800-1600 on a CEVAL-055 board · Vcc 11.5 V

Turns a voltage into a frequency. In the JACON it retransmits at the frequency the phase detector measured.

What the plots are

Grey squares: 4 October, set by hand with a bench supply, VCO straight into the analyzer. Green line: 10 October, automated, 176 points, with the splitter and mixer attached.

Power plot: 6 October, ten points read off the analyzer.

Numbers

  • Range seen: 733 MHz at 0 V to 1360 MHz at 10.1 V.
  • For 1.0 to 1.2 GHz it needs 4.8 to 7.8 V.
  • Slope in that band: about 68 MHz/V.
  • Output in band: +4.8 to +7.4 dBm, falling as frequency rises.
  • The “0.8 GHz + 60 MHz/V” line reads 48 to 97 MHz too high.
  • Listed: 0.5 to 19 V tuning, 6 ± 2 dBm out, pushing 2 MHz/V, 30 mA max.

Delay-line splitter tested

Mini-Circuits ZFRSC-42-S+ · resistive, DC to 4200 MHz

Splits the signal in two. One copy takes a longer cable. The phase detector compares them.

Numbers

  • Max input 0.75 W (28.8 dBm). It can burn 0.375 W inside.
  • Loss per output: 6.0 dB typical, 6.5 dB worst case in our band.
  • Measured 6.7 dB each side at 1.04 GHz.
  • Isolation between outputs: about 6 dB. That is low.
  • Outputs match within 0.2 dB and 3 degrees.
  • Extra cable on one side: 4.5 in, about 0.55 ns.

Math

\[ \tau = \frac{\Delta L}{v} = \frac{0.114\ \text{m}}{0.7 \times 3\times10^{8}\ \text{m/s}} \approx 0.55\ \text{ns} \]
\[ \Delta\phi = 2\pi f \tau \approx 216^\circ\ \text{at}\ 1.1\ \text{GHz} \]

The drawing asks for 0.2 ns (3 to 4 cm) to sit at 90°.

Phase detector tested

Mini-Circuits ZX05-30W-S+ · level 7 double-balanced mixer, 300 to 4000 MHz

Both splitter outputs go into the mixer, one on LO, one on RF. Same frequency on both, so the IF port gives a DC voltage that depends on the phase between them. Phase depends on frequency, so voltage tells you frequency.

What the plots are

Left: September, Pluto SDR as the source at four power settings, voltage read on a multimeter. Right: 10 October, the VCO as the source, voltage read on the scope.

Different bands, different sources, different power. They are not the same curve.

Numbers

  • Max RF power 50 mW (17 dBm). Max IF current 40 mA.
  • Wants +7 dBm on LO. Got about +0.6 dBm.
  • Pluto, −12.25 dBm setting: one-way from 1.03 to 1.20 GHz, 5 to 33 mV.
  • VCO: one-way from 744 to 788 MHz, 30 to 121 mV, about 2.7 mV/MHz.
  • At 1.08 GHz the output was 46, 26, 12 and 8 mV for −2, −6, −11 and −12.25 dBm. Power matters a lot.
  • RF port VSWR about 2.8 to 3.5 near 1 GHz. LO port 1.7 to 2.0.

Math

\[ V_\text{out} \approx V_0 + A(P)\,\cos(2\pi f \tau) \]

\(A\) grows with input power and \(V_0\) is a DC offset. So the map from voltage to frequency only holds at one power.
